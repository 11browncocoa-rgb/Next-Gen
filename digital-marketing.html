About This Course

Digital marketing helps businesses, creators, and organizations connect with people online. This beginner-friendly course introduces the core concepts of online marketing and teaches students how to build an effective digital presence.

Students will learn practical marketing strategies using social media, search engines, email, and content creation. By the end of the course, you'll understand how to reach an audience, promote products or services, and grow a brand online.

What You Will Learn

  • Introduction to digital marketing and online branding
  • Understanding your target audience
  • Creating engaging content for social media
  • Basics of Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and TikTok marketing
  • Introduction to Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
  • Email marketing fundamentals
  • Building a simple digital marketing strategy
  • Using free online marketing tools
  • Measuring results with basic analytics
  • Best practices for growing an online presence

Course Details

Level: Beginner

Duration: 6 weeks

Format: Online one-on-one or group lessons

Ideal For: Students, entrepreneurs, small business owners, content creators, and anyone interested in promoting products or services online
